## Architecture and financial provenance
FCMS currently has one company and no tenant/branch schema. Client and finance claims explicitly grant company scope; staff workforce reads remain owner/team scoped unless the actor has the appropriate expanded claim. Roles are claim bundles, never business authorization checks.

Modules own their persistence. IAM/client public directory views expose minimal read facts. Finance owns finance_gl_sources and FinancePostingSourceService. The integration dependency is Accounting → public Finance source contract; finance services must not depend back on accounting. No fee snapshot is treated as a cash receipt, no account treatment is inferred and no live opening balance is fabricated.

A posting mapping binds an operational event to two active posting accounts, voucher type, explicit cash-flow category and policy reference. Setup creates a pending revision; a different accounting reviewer approves/rejects it. Approval serializes by transaction and retires its prior approved revision atomically while retaining review evidence. Mapping configuration alone does not post operational records.

For source integration, keep original operational events and their actual reversals as separate journal evidence, even when reversal preceded GL preparation. Use exact linked GL reversals and independent review. Do not silently net away historical transactions. Preserve previous cancelled preparation attempts and prevent two posted journals for one source. Missing historical payment dates require reviewed evidence rather than guessed dates.
