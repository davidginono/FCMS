The owner's `Microfinance` checkout is the implementation reference for this replacement. The 18 November update adapts commit `e205c8b02f176f04cb18817607d96d65da59bb0b` (split vouchers and generated accounting codes). ## Canonical workspaces

- `/accounting/config/activities`: searchable, sortable, paged activities; modal create; server-generated `ACT-000001` code; immutable existing code; edit name/description; activate/deactivate. Active children must be deactivated before their parent.
- `/accounting/config/transactions`: activity-filtered register and modal creation of the transaction plus its complete template. New ordinary transactions receive a server-generated `TXN-000001` code, `MANUAL_JOURNAL` event and one TOTAL debit/credit pair; creation no longer asks for a code, event or component. Existing specialised component identities remain stored and editable only through their established component set. Automatic source adapters and historical classifications remain usable. FCMS voucher direction, cash-flow classification and policy evidence remain required.
- `/accounting/config/transactions/{id}`: current and proposed component rules, independent review, version checks and retained revisions. Editing a template creates a new proposal rather than changing approved accounting evidence.
- `/accounting/vouchers/receipts`, `/payments`, `/journals`: separate bounded registers, filters, selection-dependent View/PDF/Print and independent Create.
- `/{route}/new`: activity or manual entry; date; receipt payer/payment payee; optional FCMS client association; default money account for receipt/payment pairs; reference; cash-flow classification; optional narration/evidence; one to fifty transactions. Add debit/Add credit converts a manual pair into explicit entries with their own accounts and amounts. Each side must equal its transaction amount; duplicate accounts on one side and accounts on both sides of one transaction are rejected. Receipt debits/payment credits can span multiple active FCMS money accounts; the opposite side must be non-money. Limits are 50 entries per transaction and 500 ledger lines overall. Add/remove preserves remaining values, stable control identities and indexed submission paths. Configured mappings remain fixed until manual mode is explicitly selected.
- `/{route}/number`: CSRF-protected, actor/request-bound number reservation, local spinner, cancellation of stale date responses and explicit retry. A reservation creates no journal; gaps are possible. The committed voucher consumes the displayed number.
- POST `/{route}`: server preview without saving, or atomic draft/submission for existing FCMS independent review. Success uses PRG to the relevant register with a persistent Flashbar and record link. Field failures retain the live form and identify affected controls. Preview replaces only its result region and is cleared when inputs change. Financial commands are never automatically replayed after an uncertain response.
- `/{route}/{id}`: transaction/account snapshots, document, draft submission/cancellation, independent rejection/posting and authorized linked reversal. PDF and Print use the same A4 PDF renderer. Receipt exports list each transaction once and omit GL accounts; payment exports retain paired account rows with the money account first.

All account/template choices and registers are bounded. Posting checks active accounts and current activity/template revisions on capture, validates every money/counter-account and computes exact decimal totals. Template preparation locks all activities, transactions and revisions in deterministic order, then reads their complete rules in batches. Successful exact retries return their original voucher; changed actor/payload retries fail. A database unique index protects every money-account/reference combination, including conflicts against historical ordinary voucher evidence. The transaction amount contributes once to the voucher total regardless of entry count. Legacy paired request hashes stay unchanged; split content adds a versioned hash segment.

## Retired and retained implementations

Removed the old `ConfiguredVoucherController`, `ConfiguredVoucherService`, `ConfiguredVoucherForm`, single-row configured form, manual journal form and old journal register/detail JSPs. The old journal create/edit/save and singular configured-voucher routes have no entry handler. The unused posting-mapping redirect/command facade is removed. No alternative mutation endpoint bypasses the replacement form.

`JournalService.createDraft` and `JournalForm` remain the creation-only shared ledger adapter used by source posting, historical corrections and the replacement voucher service. `PostingMappingService` remains shared with finance source adapters. `JournalsController` retains historical list/read, review, reversal and PDF routes because dated financial-statement drills, source detail pages and existing journal/reversal links identify those destinations. They render the replacement workspace; historical documents use an explicit read-only fragment. `gl_configured_vouchers` is retained as immutable historical evidence and has no mutable line editor. The dead journal-form builder, update/delete-lines branch, version input, posting-mapping list service, component add/remove UI, exclusive editor labels and transaction-code input DTO field have been removed. These are required active financial/history dependencies, not fallback creation workflows.

V40 prevents insert/update/delete of ledger lines once a new or historical configured voucher envelope has been captured, including unposted evidence. Shared source preparation and new linked reversals keep their existing line-insertion path.

V39 adds immutable explicit entry evidence and a shared postings view that reads both historical pairs and new split entries; enforces row balance, exact ledger agreement and complete money-reference reservations; raises the retained posting guard to 500 lines; and creates code sequences seeded beyond matching stored identities. No stored codes, account money flags, financial evidence or historical retry hashes are rewritten. FCMS uses its existing `cash_account` authority; Microfinance purpose/loan-family infrastructure is not transplanted.

V38 declares generated foreign keys for all five FCMS client categories, preserving the client snapshot and allowing the client-owned lifecycle guard to detect and protect voucher references. Partial indexes support those actual reference checks.

## Migration and operations

It does not delete or rewrite financial/audit history or released migrations. Deferred checks verify complete row count, exact totals, debit/credit pairs, selected component rules and consumed number against the journal, including later state changes. Deploy the matching application and forward migration together. Do not drop the new evidence tables to roll back. An older reader limited to 50 or 100 lines is unsuitable for a split voucher; a rollback must retain a compatible complete reader and existing review/posting safeguards.

## Activity-selected versus Manual entry

The activity selector owns the voucher entry mode. A configured row shows only its activity-filtered transaction selector and amount; its approved template supplies accounts and the default narration. There is no per-row manual-mode button or redundant header money-account picker. Choosing Manual entry shows narration and starts each transaction with one debit and one credit account/amount row. Add debit/Add credit appends explicit entries immediately, without a pair-to-split conversion step. At least one entry on each side remains.

Manual row amounts are computed from debit entries using exact cents in the browser and independently using BigDecimal in the service. The aggregate amount control is hidden and excluded from the manual POST. Both sides must balance; the service still validates account eligibility, cash-flow classification, limits, authorization, idempotency and immutable capture. Configured narration defaults to the approved transaction name. Legacy paired payloads and their original headers remain accepted for required shared workflows and compatible retries; the new UI does not render their old account controls. No schema migration is required for this presentation change.
